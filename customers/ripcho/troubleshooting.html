Troubleshooting

Use the smallest safe fix first. Preserve job data, record what changed, and escalate when identity, order data, or production output is uncertain.

Duplicate or incorrect subject record

Stop production for the affected subject. Compare source data, image, class/teacher information, and the original job record. Repair only after the correct record is confirmed; recheck affected output before release.

Background tint or wrong background

Confirm the required background from the current workflow or job instruction. Do not rerun output until image treatment and the correct subject record are verified.

Queue, bitmap, or output issue

Confirm the job selected, destination queue, product/output setting, and count. Test one item when appropriate. If a repeatable system issue remains, capture the exact message and notify the responsible lead.

Composite import mismatch

Check field mapping, source data, unmatched records, and quantity before printing. Correct source/mapping issues before producing a full run.

Missing package or service item

Compare the order to the current flyer and job configuration. Do not substitute a product based on memory; escalate an unclear mapping.

When you escalate: include job name, subject/order affected, time, output queue, steps already tried, and a screenshot of the message or setting when possible.

Training path

Training center

A practical route for a new Ripcho operator. Train with a lead, follow the workflows in order, and use progress sheets as the record of completed work.

New operator checklist

  1. Learn the production flow: job review → image preparation → output → packing → closeout.
  2. Review the UG / CP workflow and observe a complete job with an experienced operator.
  3. Learn how progress sheets, flyers, and package references control the work.
  4. Practice queue verification and first-piece inspection before releasing output.
  5. Complete a supervised retake, then review the troubleshooting and escalation process.
  6. Train on the workflow(s) assigned to the operator: Spring, Sports, and/or Composites.

Terms to know

TermWorking meaning
Progress sheetThe job’s production checklist and completion record.
Service itemA nonstandard or supplemental product requiring its own output/handling path.
First-piece checkInspecting the first finished item before committing a full batch.
EscalationPausing work and getting a lead’s decision when data, identity, order, or output is uncertain.

Printing & service items

Route each product to the correct queue only after the job and package configuration have passed review.

Output areaUseControl point
NoritsuPhotographic print outputVerify queue, job selection, print count, and first-output quality.
KonicaPress products and compositesConfirm approved media/tray, finish settings, and copies before release.
Dye SubApproved specialty/photo productsCheck the assigned output path, product setup, and first finished item.
Labels, stickers & directoriesService-item outputVerify sorting, subject matching, and page-break/output settings before the run.
First-piece rule: Inspect the first completed item whenever a job, product, stock, or queue changes. It is faster to stop one incorrect run than to rework a full batch.

Release checklist

  1. Confirm the current job and correct workflow.
  2. Verify approved product, paper/media, queue, and quantity.
  3. Check first output for correct subject, color, trim, personalization, and count.
  4. Record output completion on the progress sheet; flag reruns immediately.

Production workflow

Retakes

Retakes are a controlled extension of the original job—not a separate order flow. Preserve the original job data and move only verified retake work through production.

Do not overwrite the original job. Keep retake images and processing distinct until they have been checked and merged according to the job’s approved process.
  1. Confirm the original job and identify the subjects being retaken.
  2. Create or use the designated retake job/work area; verify it contains retake images only.
  3. Complete needed background work and retouching, then inspect each subject against the original order data.
  4. Merge approved retake work back into the original job using the established job process.
  5. Reprocess only the affected orders and release only the retake output to the appropriate queues.
  6. Record the completion and any missing or unmatched subjects on the job progress record.

Common checks

Duplicate or unmatched subject

Stop before printing. Confirm identity, school/class information, and the correct original subject record. Use the Duplicate Records reference in Troubleshooting when a repair is required.

Background does not match

Verify the approved background selection before merging or rerunning output. Do not release a retake based on an assumed background.

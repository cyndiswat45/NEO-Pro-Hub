Current production workflow

UG retakes

Process retakes as a controlled addendum to the original UG job. Keep retake images and orders separate until the retake TNJ is verified and merged.

Protect the Day 1 job. Do not replace original job files when copying retake images. Confirm that only retake orders and images are in the retake job before merging anything into the original.

Download the current retake progress sheet

1. Confirm the retake job

  1. Create the RUG work order and add the verified delivery address.
  2. Duplicate the TNJ in FTP. Open the _RE job and confirm only retake orders are present and every retake image is in the _Retake folder.
  3. Record the retake image count for color-correction billing. Compare paper and online totals against the work order before upload, then save the TNJ.
  4. Open the original Day 1 UG job. Copy the retake #Images folder into the original job's MySQL folder without replacing existing original images.

2. Merge and reprocess

  1. In the original job, delete all existing orders in Orders mode, then close and reopen to confirm they are gone.
  2. Import / merge the retake TNJ into the original job. Use the established import behavior for expected missing images: hold Shift and choose Ignore.
  3. In Details mode, change the offer to the approved SchoolName_R retake offer. Reprocess orders against that offer.
  4. Compare paper and online totals after import with the work order. Stop and investigate any mismatch before output.

3. Retouch and color correct

  1. In Orders mode, retouch the requested images and record X1–X4 totals.
  2. In Images mode, filter/sort retouched work and refresh the updated images. Return to Orders mode and use the green check to clear completed retouch orders.
  3. In Images mode, view the _RETAKE folder and color correct retake images. Review the rest of the job only as a safety check; original images should already be correct.

4. Print retake output

Sort rule: Folder / Subject is not appropriate for retakes. Use the approved sort—commonly Grade / Teacher / Name—and apply it to both the print queue and retake proof-card set.
  1. Create a new _Retake_Proof Card item sheet. Match its sort order to the print queue and add the proof-card quantity for billing.
  2. If a Class Picture is not visible, reset home folders in Details mode before continuing.
  3. Print required magnets, Dye Sub products, Dye Sub labels, digital downloads, and Noritsu output. Record counts as each queue finishes.
  4. Use the current printing guide for the approved Noritsu, Dye Sub, sticker, directory, and label settings.

5. Bill, pack, ship, and verify IOS

  1. Review Order Statistics for retouch totals and Queue Statistics for printed output. Update the LabWorks work order and verify shipping details.
  2. Complete the printing and billing production-node/checkered-flag steps.
  3. Quality-check while packing for dust lines, white spots, and color issues. Confirm every requested service item is finished; Dye Sub goes in the envelope except mugs.
  4. For shipping, add newsprint, keep work order and invoice out of the box, scan the invoice barcode, match email and UPS address, print the UPS label, and record the ship date.
  5. Complete IOS upload and IOS check before final closeout.

When to stop and escalate

Original job files appear to be changing

Stop. Confirm you are working in the retake folder and that no original images or original order data have been replaced.

Order totals do not match

Do not print. Recheck paper and online counts in the work order, retake TNJ, and original job after reprocessing.

Proof cards and print queue do not sort the same way

Do not release output. Correct the sort before printing; retake packing depends on those two sets matching.

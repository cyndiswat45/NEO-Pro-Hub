Production workflow

UG / CP workflow

Use this guide for Underclass / Class Picture production. Keep the job progress sheet with the job and record exceptions as they are found.

Stop and verify before output. Confirm the job, offer/package setup, image count, and any required service items before releasing production files.

Workflow at a glance

  1. Review the work order, customer instructions, and current progress sheet.
  2. Import or open the job and confirm the expected subjects and order data are present.
  3. Check the offer, package setup, duplicates, background requirements, and retouch notes.
  4. Refresh images and complete approved retouching before releasing print output.
  5. Route photographic, press, Dye Sub, label, and service-item work to the appropriate production queue.
  6. Use the progress sheet to verify packing, billing, and final job closeout.

Control points

Before printingVerify package/order data, retouch status, background treatment, and that the correct job—not a test or retake copy—is selected.
Before packingMatch finished product and service items to the job’s order information. Keep exceptions with the job until resolved.
At closeoutComplete the job progress record and flag missing output, reruns, or billing exceptions.

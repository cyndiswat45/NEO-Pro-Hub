Production workflow

Sports / Memory Mates

Sports jobs combine individual images, team images, and personalized products. Validate the job’s current product setup before output.

  1. Review the job scope, team photo requirements, and school/league-specific flyer.
  2. Confirm individual and team image availability before creating Memory Mate or related output.
  3. Verify personalization, package/service items, and any approved retouch notes.
  4. Route photographic output, personalized items, and specialty products to the correct queue.
  5. Inspect complete orders for the right individual, team, personalization, and quantity before packing.

Variant control

Some schools use different sports flyer configurations. Treat the job-specific flyer as authoritative, especially when an optional product or ticket item is not offered for that school.

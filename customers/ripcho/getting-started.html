Start a Ripcho Job

Use this short preflight before production begins. It makes sure the job is routed to the right workflow and that the operator starts with the correct working materials.

Do this before opening a production workflow. The job progress sheet and current job materials are the working source of truth. This page helps you choose the right procedure; it does not replace the job-specific sheet.

1. Identify the job type

UG / CP

Underclass or class-picture production, including standard order processing and packing.

Retakes

Any job that must be isolated from and then merged back into an original job.

Spring Portraits

Spring portrait work, including the spring-specific order and background steps.

Sports / Memory Mates

Individual and team sports production, including specialty items.

Composites

Composite production with its required data, quantity, and print checks.

2. Gather the working materials

  1. Locate the correct current progress sheet for the job type and school.
  2. Confirm the work order, TNJ/job files, images, graphics, and any customer-specific instructions are available.
  3. Review the job for special products or service items: directories, stickers, labels, Dye Sub items, retouching, or digital products.
  4. Open the relevant customer flyer or product reference if package contents need to be confirmed.

Open Ripcho forms, progress sheets, and package flyers

3. Set the job up for a clean handoff

  1. Write down or verify the school name, job name, job type, due date, and operator.
  2. Follow the selected workflow in order. Do not skip its validation points because they protect order counts, output, and packing.
  3. Keep the progress sheet with the job and mark each completed checkpoint as work moves through production.
  4. When the workflow reaches a printing or specialty-output step, use the documented settings before sending the full job.
Stop and ask before proceeding if the job type is unclear, the progress sheet does not match the materials, an expected folder or offer is missing, or package/output counts do not reconcile.
